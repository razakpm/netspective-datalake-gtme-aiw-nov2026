## Implementation Strategy

- **MVP first**: Phases 1 and 2 then US1. This gives a defensible, explained recommendation for an account, deterministic and versioned, with the safety rules (suppression, consent, exclusion, capacity) proven by the corpus before any form is opened to the public. Stop and validate V1 to V14.
- **Incremental delivery**: add US2 (the only prospect-facing entry point and acceptance Scenario C), then US3 (Scenario G), then US4 to US6 (each independent), then the screens, the always-on scenario test and the optional browser test.
- **Test-first throughout**: the routing corpus (T005) and the field-name fixture (T006) exist before the code they test; a rule set with a failing case cannot be activated.
- **Business inputs**: the register file and approved notice wording come from the business. Until a notice version is approved the public form returns 503; tests use synthetic files and a synthetic notice only, and nothing synthetic is seeded into a real environment.
- **Scope guard**: nothing in this feature sends an email or message, books a session, builds the website, qualifies a lead or creates a next best action; those are features 007, 009 and 012.
